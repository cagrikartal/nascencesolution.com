You stay in charge of the money

An agent that spends money needs limits it can't talk its way around. These are built in, not settings it can change.

  • Your account, your card

    Ads run on your own Google Ads account and Google bills you directly. We never hold your ad money and never add a markup.

  • A limit it can't raise

    It works within the monthly budget you set. It can't raise that limit, connect an account or add a payment method.

  • Autopilot, or your approval

    Let it publish on its own, or have every ad and post wait for your approval first.

  • Nothing made up

    It writes from the business profile you confirmed. It won't invent reviews, quotes or results.

  • Logins kept off the page

    Access to your accounts is stored encrypted on our servers and never sent to a browser.

How it starts

  1. Point it at your website

    It reads your own pages and drafts a profile of your business: what you sell, who to, and where.

  2. Check the profile

    Correct anything it got wrong. It won't act on a profile you haven't confirmed.

  3. Set your budget

    Choose a monthly ad budget and connect your Google Ads account.

  4. It gets to work

    Each week it writes, publishes, measures and shifts effort toward what's working. Each month it re-plans the split.
