It doesn't write one ad. It runs an experiment.

Most AI tools write a single ad and publish it. This agent writes several that differ on purpose, then moves your budget toward the version customers actually respond to. Every week it gets a clearer picture of what works for your kind of business.

Tested before it touches your money: against a market simulator built from published 2026 advertising benchmarks, the agent captured on average 83.5% of the gap between choosing ads at random and the best option available (five runs, 74% to 91%). This is a simulation, not a result from a real business.

How it starts

  1. Point it at your website

    It reads your own pages and drafts a profile of your business: what you sell, who to, and where.

  2. Check the profile

    Correct anything it got wrong. It won't act on a profile you haven't confirmed.

  3. Set your budget

    Choose a monthly ad budget and connect your Google Ads account.

  4. It gets to work

    Each week it writes, publishes, measures and shifts effort toward what's working. Each month it re-plans how the budget is split.

Who it's for

Owner-run UK businesses spending roughly £100 to £500 a month on marketing, who can't justify an agency: trades and home services, professional services, hospitality, independent shops and small online stores.
