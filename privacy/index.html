Privacy notice

Last updated 8 October 2026

This notice explains what happens to the details you give us when you join the Nascence waitlist. Nascence Solution ("we") decides how those details are used.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your business website, if you choose to give it.
  • The date and time you joined.

The services named below also process technical data, such as your IP address, to deliver this website and receive the form.

Why we use it

Only to contact you about the Nascence pilot and launch, and to understand what kinds of businesses are interested. We use your details because you gave them to us for this purpose (your consent). We won't sell them or use them for anything else.

Who else handles it

The waitlist is stored in Google Forms, in our own Google account. The website is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Both may store data in the United States. We don't use any other service to process your details.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or 12 months after Nascence launches, whichever comes first.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent at any time, by emailing cagri@nascencesolution.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
